Overview

Building the systems behind a stylized Italian world.

Vitis: A Wine Country Tale is a long-term Unity project centered on building a stylized world inspired by the Italian countryside.

The current production phase is heavily focused on world building. I am developing custom shaders, a climate system and authoring tools for terrain, environments and procedural workflows.

01

World Building Tools

Terrain authoring, painting, scatter, procedural placement and environment iteration.

02

Shaders & Rendering

Stylized shaders, pixel-inspired rendering, environmental materials and visual systems.

03

Climate System

Day, atmosphere, weather and environmental controls designed to support world mood and iteration.
