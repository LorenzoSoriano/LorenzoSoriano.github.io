OVERVIEW

Building the systems behind a long-term stylized world.

Vitis: A Wine Country Tale is a long-term Unity project centered on building a stylized world inspired by the Italian countryside.

The current production phase is focused heavily on world building. I am developing custom shaders, a climate system and world-building tools for terrain, environment authoring and procedural workflows. The project is intentionally long-term, with systems being developed and refined alongside the world itself.

01

World Building Tools

Custom workflows for terrain authoring, painting, scatter, procedural placement and environment iteration.

02

Shaders & Rendering

Stylized shaders, pixel-inspired rendering, environmental materials and visual systems built for a cohesive world.

03

Climate System

Day, atmosphere, weather and environmental controls designed to support world mood and long-term iteration.
