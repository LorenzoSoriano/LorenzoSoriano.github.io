COMMUNICATION & COLLABORATION

Clear ideas, readable documentation and productive collaboration.

Good development work is not only about making a system work: it also means making decisions understandable to the people working around it.

APPROACH

Make complex work easier to understand and easier to act on.

I try to communicate technical and design choices in a way that gives the team enough context to make decisions without adding unnecessary complexity.

That means adapting the level of detail to the audience, documenting useful information and keeping feedback connected to concrete goals rather than personal preference.

01

Documentation

Organising requirements, design decisions and technical notes so they remain useful during iteration instead of becoming passive archives.

02

Presentation of Ideas

Explaining systems and design intent clearly through examples, references, diagrams and concise written communication.

03

Collaboration & Feedback

Working across disciplines, comparing constraints and using feedback to improve the shared result without losing the project goal.
