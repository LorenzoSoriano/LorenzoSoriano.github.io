ABOUT

I like working where technical decisions and creative decisions affect each other.

I’m Lorenzo Soriano, a game developer with a multidisciplinary background spanning Unity development, 3D art and game design.

My work focuses on creating systems that are useful in production and still serve a clear visual or design goal. I’m especially interested in world building, technical art, tools and interactive environments.

This portfolio is a living collection of projects and problems I’ve worked on, with an emphasis on process and contribution rather than long lists of software.
