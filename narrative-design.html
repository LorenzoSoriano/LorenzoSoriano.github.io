NARRATIVE DESIGN

Turning themes and ideas into readable player experiences.

I am interested in how narrative can emerge through structure, space, pacing and interaction rather than relying only on exposition.

APPROACH

Story works best when it is part of the experience.

My approach starts from the player journey: what the player understands, when information arrives and how spaces, objectives and interactions reinforce the tone of the experience.

I use narrative thinking as a design tool to keep theme, pacing and gameplay aligned, especially when a project needs to communicate clearly with limited time or limited exposition.

01

Structure & Pacing

Organising beats, reveals and changes of rhythm so the experience has a clear progression and does not lose focus.

02

Environmental Storytelling

Using spaces, landmarks, props and level flow to communicate context without interrupting play.

03

Narrative Through Interaction

Connecting player actions and mechanics to the theme so interaction carries part of the narrative meaning.
